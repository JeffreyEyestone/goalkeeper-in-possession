# AI-Assisted Research Disclosure - Recommended Text

Generative AI-assisted tools (OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex, and Anthropic Claude) were used during study development and manuscript preparation for research planning, code generation and review, debugging, robustness-test design, literature discovery, document drafting and editorial revision. The human author retained responsibility for scientific definitions, data exclusions, methodological choices, statistical interpretation, source and citation verification, and final manuscript text. All numerical results reported in the study are generated from reproducible code and canonical evidence files; AI-generated prose or calculations were not treated as empirical evidence. No AI system is listed as an author, and no synthetic research observations were used.
